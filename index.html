v10 — Dynamic Non-Cubic Interiors, Full High School, Lived-In Sweet Tooth & Regional Atlas Map
Current Build

• Fixed the root cause of black/green wall tiles appearing behind desks, beds, chairs, and pews (dedicated invisible furniture collision layer).
• Upgraded interiors to dynamic, Stardew-style connected wings & hallways surrounded by clean auto-tiled contours (no more flat cubes!).
• Expanded Colorado Springs High School into a full multi-room zone (Locker Hallway, closed Physics/Latin/Gym doors, AP Calculus Rm 102, Trophy Case & Student Lounge).
• Revamped Sweet Tooth Candy Store with a staff service counter behind Chloe, seating, customers, gumball machine, and a melting dropped ice cream cone in the corner.
• Replaced the blocky mini-map with a smooth, high-resolution Regional Atlas showing both Westside (left) and East Colorado Springs (right) with Fog of War.

• Upgraded dialogue portraits to 96×96 high-detail, book-accurate character art (Jacob's dirty-blond hair & glacier-blue eyes, Cyrine's platinum hair, Letonin's hoop earring & honey-brown eyes, Death's black hoodie, Navi's long wavy hair, etc.).
• Reformed overworld & battle character sprites with rounded heads, contoured shoulders, and natural proportions (no more blocky limbs).
• Kept Stardew-style multi-room interior layouts while re-theming walls/floors to match the modern Colorado Springs urban-fantasy aesthetic of the novel.
• Added this subtle Release Updates changelog link at the bottom.
